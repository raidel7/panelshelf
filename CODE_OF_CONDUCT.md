# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small project about reading comics on a NAS;
nothing here is worth being unpleasant over.

## What is expected

- Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good faith.
- Criticise code and decisions, not people.
- Accept that "no" is a legitimate answer, and that a maintainer working in
  their spare time may be slow or may decline something reasonable.
- Respect that people run different hardware, have different needs, and
  organise their libraries differently from you.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or wearing people down by repetition.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else someone
is representing the project.

## Reporting

If someone is behaving this way, contact the maintainer directly using the
address on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately.

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete comments, and may block accounts, when
that is the proportionate response. Serious or repeated behaviour gets a
permanent block without a warning cycle.
